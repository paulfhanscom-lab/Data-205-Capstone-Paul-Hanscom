#EDA
##Explanation
For EDA I first loaded my weather dataset and looked at its structure, head, and a summary of the totalprcp variable. I then created a histogram to visualize distribution of the variable totalprcp. Because of its heavy right skew which is to be anticipated when dealing with percipitation data I decided to graph the log of total percipitation to better visualize the distribution. I also visualized the structure of my crash data and created a line graph of crashes overtime. there is no clear trend between crashes and time.
